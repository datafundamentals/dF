⚠️ ANTI-PATTERN - DO NOT REPLICATE

Educational Only: Auth Emulator Demo

DO NOT COPY THIS PATTERN into other apps in the monorepo!

This demo proves that Firebase Auth emulator is possible, but it's an anti-pattern for our architecture. Here's why:

  • Creates unnecessary differentiation between apps
  • Doesn't match production authentication flow
  • Auth emulator has known issues and limitations
  • Makes apps harder to understand and maintain

✅ Instead, use df-auth-wrapper with production Google Sign-In for all authentication needs. See the canonical pattern that you SHOULD copy for your apps.
